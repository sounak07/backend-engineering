## Salesforce MTS, SMTS. LLD & HLD Interview Questions (Compiled)

Compiled from interview-experience sites, LeetCode Discuss, Reddit/Blind, Glassdoor, and Medium posts (mostly 2024–2026, India offices — Bangalore/Hyderabad). Salesforce's design round is usually a **single combined round** that starts as HLD and drops into LLD (class diagrams / code) for the last 20–30 minutes, though sometimes it's split into two separate rounds. Design rounds are typically run by an **LMTS or SMTS** interviewer; the Hiring Manager round is separate and behavioral/architecture-discussion focused.

## 3. Combined / ambiguous LLD+HLD rounds (worth knowing as a pattern)

Several candidates report Salesforce deliberately blends the two:

- **Notification service**: HLD (architecture, DB) for ~40 min → LLD (class diagrams, APIs, edge cases, patterns) for the rest. ([LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/discuss/interview-experience/6321318/Salesforce-or-SMTS-or-Awaited/))
- **Sign-up/Login at billion-user scale**: HLD approach selection (bloom filter) → full LLD of the same system. ([LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/discuss/interview-experience/6934266/))
- **Job Scheduler**: explicitly described as "covered both LLD + HLD aspects" in one sitting. ([LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/discuss/post/6857467/salesforce-interview-experience-lmts-apr-a9rw/))
- **Message Queue**: interviewer gives only requirements, expects candidate to _identify_ the system itself, then goes deep on internals before pulling back out to "how does this fit a larger system" (HLD framing). ([Studocu](https://www.studocu.com/in/document/jawaharlal-nehru-technological-university-kakinada/electronics-and-communication-engineering/salesforce-mts-interview-experience-summary-preparation-guide/149337073))

**Takeaway for prep:** Don't silo LLD-only or HLD-only practice — Salesforce interviewers frequently pivot from one to the other in the _same_ 60–90 minute slot, and often ask you to connect the design back to a real system you've built (a good fit for narrating your Raft AI architecture work).

## 4. Recurring themes across LLD prompts

- **Concurrency / double-booking / thread-safety** shows up constantly (BookMyShow double booking, Meeting Scheduler simultaneous booking, thread-safe task scheduler, connection pool). Practicing locking strategies and race-condition handling for booking/reservation systems is high-leverage.
- **Design pattern fluency** is explicitly probed (Observer, Singleton, Strategy-style extensibility for Splitwise's expense types).
- **Follow-up "adapt this to Kafka" style pivots** — interviewers like extending a clean pattern (e.g., Observer) into a messaging/streaming context.
- **Ambiguous prompts by design** (Login System with no clarifications allowed, Message Queue where you must infer the system) — signals they're testing requirement-gathering and comfort with ambiguity, not just pattern recall.

## 5. Recurring themes across HLD prompts

- **Ride-sharing / booking / marketplace systems** (Uber, BookMyShow) — matching, surge pricing, double-booking prevention.
- **Rate limiting and scalability infrastructure** (distributed rate limiter, HA/DR at scale) — reflects Salesforce's own platform-scale concerns.
- **Notification/messaging fan-out systems** — asked more than once across different candidates.
- **"Design X-like-Google/Facebook" prompts** (Docs, News Feed, Search) tied back to candidate's own resume domain when possible.
- Interviewers frequently ask you to **map the design back to your current company's architecture** — a strong argument for having a crisp, well-rehearsed narrative of your Raft AI systems (workflow engine, EAV ingestion, rule evaluator) ready to plug into any HLD discussion.

## 6. Round-structure notes (useful for calibrating prep)

- Typical loop: **OA (HackerRank, 2 DSA problems, ~90 min, ~80% cutoff)** → **DSA round(s)** → **LLD round** (sometimes LLD+HLD combined) → separate **HLD round** in some loops → **Hiring Manager round** (behavioral + "explain your project's architecture" + LP-style questions).
- SMTS loops more consistently include a dedicated design round than MTS loops (some MTS candidates on Blind report _no_ system design at all, only 2× DSA + HM — but this is team-dependent and not guaranteed).
- Design round duration is usually **45–90 minutes**; several sources explicitly note the round can run over and get cut short.
- Interviewer level for design rounds is typically **LMTS or SMTS**; the final round is often a **Director or Sr. Engineering Manager**.
- Process is **not standardized across teams** — several Blind commenters explicitly warn "every team does it differently," so treat this compilation as a strong prior, not a guarantee.
